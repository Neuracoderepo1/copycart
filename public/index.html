— THE IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Know before
you follow.

CopyCart is an early prototype. Here’s what it does — and what it doesn’t do yet.

Is this connected to a live exchange?

No. This is a demo-data build. It has no exchange API connection, live market feed, user account system, or trade execution capability.

Are these real traders or verified returns?

No. All profiles, scores, and profile details shown are illustrative sample data. They are not real people, verified track records, or live rankings.

Does a higher score mean I will make money?

No. A score cannot predict future results or remove market risk. Past performance does not guarantee future returns. Crypto assets can be highly volatile, and you can lose your entire investment.

Can I automatically copy trades right now?

No. Trade copying is not enabled. Secure exchange integration, permission controls, user consent, limits, monitoring, and a security review would be required before release.

How will CopyCart score traders in production?

The intended framework considers consistency, drawdown and volatility, track-record depth, and risk discipline. The formula, weights, data provenance, verification process, and anti-manipulation safeguards still need implementation and validation.
